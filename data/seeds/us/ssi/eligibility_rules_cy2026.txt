SUPPLEMENTAL SECURITY INCOME (SSI) — POLICY SEED
Source: 20 CFR Part 416; SSA "Cost-of-Living Increase and Other Determinations for 2026" (90 FR 49047)
For use in rationale trace generation. Not a complete regulatory text.

=== CATEGORICAL ELIGIBILITY (20 CFR 416.202) ===
Aged (65 or older), blind, or disabled; plus citizenship/residence and other non-financial rules.
A non-blind disabled applicant working at substantial gainful activity is not disabled
(20 CFR 416.920(b)). SGA 2026: $1,690/month (non-blind).

=== FEDERAL BENEFIT RATE, 2026 ===
$994/month eligible individual; $1,491/month eligible couple; $498 essential person.

=== COUNTABLE INCOME ===
1. $20 general exclusion, applied to unearned income first, any remainder to earned income
   (20 CFR 416.1124(c)(12)).
2. Earned income: exclude $65, then one-half of the rest (20 CFR 416.1112(c)(5), (c)(7)).
Payment = FBR - countable income. No payment if countable income >= FBR.

=== RESOURCES (20 CFR 416.1205) ===
$2,000 individual; $3,000 couple.

Not covered here: state supplementary payments, in-kind support and maintenance, deeming.
