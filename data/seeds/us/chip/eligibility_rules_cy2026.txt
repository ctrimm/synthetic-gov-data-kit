CHILDREN'S MEDICAID AND CHIP — POLICY SEED
Source: 42 CFR 435.118, 435.603; 42 CFR 457.310; KFF/Georgetown CCF state limits (January 2026)
For use in rationale trace generation. Not a complete regulatory text.

=== INCOME TEST ===
Household MAGI (42 CFR 435.603) as a percent of the 2026 HHS poverty guidelines.
Published state limits are MAGI-converted and already include the 5-percentage-point
disregard, so they are compared directly with MAGI.

=== ORDER OF DETERMINATION ===
1. Children ages 0-18 only. Each state sets a Medicaid limit per age group
   (infants 0-1, children 1-5, children 6-18).
2. MAGI at or below the Medicaid limit -> Medicaid, whether or not the child has
   other coverage.
3. Above the Medicaid limit, CHIP covers only children with no other health
   coverage (42 CFR 457.310(b)(2)), either:
   - through Medicaid paid with CHIP funds (Medicaid-expansion CHIP), or
   - through a separate CHIP program, up to the state's upper limit.

=== PRESET STATES (CY2026, % FPL) ===
Virginia:   Medicaid 148% (all ages); separate CHIP to 205%.
California: Medicaid 208% / 142% / 133% (0-1 / 1-5 / 6-18); CHIP-funded Medicaid to 266%.
Texas:      Medicaid 203% / 149% / 138%; separate CHIP to 206%.
Maryland:   Medicaid 194% / 138% / 133%; CHIP-funded Medicaid to 322%.
